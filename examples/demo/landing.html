Interactive code mapswith evidence you can trace.每条断言都有迹可循的交互式证据图谱。

Turn a codebase directory and a business question — or a described non-code business process — into a single interactive, evidence-backed HTML map with explicit evidence states. 将代码库与业务问题，或一段口述的非代码业务流程，转换为单个可交互的 HTML 证据图谱，每条断言都显式标注证据状态。

Evidence status 证据状态

  • order-service.js:createOrder [5-5]
  • verified已验证all evidence verified + inspected lines 全部证据已验证且行已检查
  • inferred推断mixed or strong-but-unproven evidence 证据混合或有力但未证实
  • unconfirmed待确认no verified evidence 无已验证证据
